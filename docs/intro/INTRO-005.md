# 1. Purpose

This document introduces the Architecture Layers of DevinaOS.

Architecture Layers describe how the Enterprise Operating System is organized from philosophy to enterprise intelligence. Each layer has a distinct responsibility while remaining connected to every other layer through governance, traceability, and Enterprise Memory.

This document provides a conceptual understanding of the layered architecture before readers explore the individual architecture series.

---

# 2. Scope

This document describes:

- The concept of Architecture Layers
- The responsibilities of each layer
- The relationship between layers
- The flow of enterprise knowledge
- The role of each layer within the Enterprise Operating System

This document does not define implementation details or technical architecture.

# 4. What are Architecture Layers?

Architecture Layers organize the Enterprise Operating System into a logical sequence of responsibilities.

Each layer answers a different architectural question while providing the foundation for the layers that follow.

Together, these layers transform enterprise philosophy into governed operations, enterprise knowledge, and continuous organizational intelligence.

# 6. Layer Responsibilities

## Philosophy Layer

Defines the identity, purpose, and long-term vision of the enterprise.

Representative Families:

- FOUNDATION
- HISTORY
- PHIL
- PRIN
- MAXIM

---

## Governance Layer

Defines the rules that guide enterprise evolution.

Representative Families:

- POL
- STD
- GL
- DOC
- REVIEW

---

## Architecture Layer

Defines the structural blueprint of the enterprise.

Representative Families:

- ARCH
- ADR
- MAP
- META
- BLUE

---

## Business Layer

Defines what the enterprise does.

Representative Families:

- DOMAIN
- CAP
- MOD
- SRV
- PARTY

---

## Operational Layer

Defines how enterprise capabilities are executed.

Representative Families:

- WF
- SOP
- WI

---

## Information Layer

Captures and governs enterprise information assets.

Representative Families:

- DATA
- REG
- ONTO
- FIN
- LEARN

---

## Intelligence Layer

Transforms enterprise knowledge into organizational intelligence.

Future capabilities include:

- Enterprise Memory
- Knowledge Graph
- AI Agents
- Semantic Search
- Decision Intelligence

# 7. Layer Relationships

The Architecture Layers form a continuous enterprise flow.

```text
Philosophy
      │
      ▼
Governance
      │
      ▼
Architecture
      │
      ▼
Business
      │
      ▼
Operations
      │
      ▼
Information
      │
      ▼

      │
      ▼

```

Each layer depends upon the guidance provided by the layers above while contributing knowledge to the layers below.

---

# 8. Design Characteristics

The Architecture Layers are designed to be:

- Hierarchical
- Governed
- Traceable
- Modular
- Technology-Agnostic
- AI-Ready
- Enterprise-Scalable
- Continuously Evolving

These characteristics enable DevinaOS to evolve without compromising architectural integrity.

---

# 9. Closing Statement

Architecture Layers provide the structural perspective of the Enterprise Operating System.

While Architecture Families organize enterprise knowledge by responsibility, Architecture Layers organize the enterprise by function and architectural dependency.

Together, they provide two complementary views of the same Enterprise Operating System.

---

> **Architecture Families describe responsibility. Architecture Layers describe flow. Together, they define the Enterprise Operating System.**
