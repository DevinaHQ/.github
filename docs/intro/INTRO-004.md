# 1. Purpose

This document introduces the Architecture Families of DevinaOS.

Architecture Families organize the Enterprise Operating System into logical architectural domains. Each family has a distinct responsibility while remaining connected through governance, traceability, and Enterprise Memory.

This document provides an overview of the Architecture Families before readers explore the individual architecture series.

---

# 2. Scope

This document describes:

- The concept of Architecture Families
- The purpose of each family
- The organization of the Enterprise Operating System
- The relationship between architecture families

This document does not define the detailed architecture of any individual family.

# 4. What are Architecture Families?

Architecture Families are the primary organizational structure of the DevinaOS Enterprise Operating System.

Rather than grouping documents by technology, department, or project, DevinaOS organizes documentation according to architectural responsibility.

Each Architecture Family represents a distinct domain of enterprise knowledge.

Together, these families establish a common architectural language for DevinaOS.

# 11. Why Architecture Families?

Architecture Families provide several benefits:

- Clear separation of responsibilities
- Consistent documentation organization
- Enterprise-wide traceability
- Easier navigation
- Scalable enterprise growth
- Technology independence
- AI-readable enterprise knowledge

Rather than becoming a collection of disconnected documents, DevinaOS grows through a structured and governed architecture.
