# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Legal Architecture for DevinaOS.

It defines the philosophy, principles, architectural foundations, governance model, and enterprise legal domains required to ensure that legal considerations are embedded throughout the lifecycle of every enterprise capability, digital asset, business process, AI agent, software component, and knowledge artifact.

LEGAL-000 serves as the master architectural document for all legal-related enterprise documentation within the DevinaOS ecosystem.

---

# 2. Vision

To establish a trustworthy, transparent, legally resilient, and AI-ready enterprise where legal governance becomes an integral part of enterprise architecture rather than a reactive support function.

---

# 3. Mission

The mission of Enterprise Legal Architecture is to:

- Integrate legal considerations into enterprise architecture.
- Govern ownership of enterprise assets.
- Ensure compliance throughout the enterprise lifecycle.
- Protect intellectual and digital assets.
- Establish legal trust across people, processes, information, software, AI, and enterprise services.
- Provide a legal foundation for enterprise automation and AI agents.

---

# 4. Philosophy

DevinaOS adopts the principle that legal governance is not merely a compliance function but a fundamental architectural capability.

Legal considerations **SHALL** be designed into enterprise capabilities from the beginning of their lifecycle rather than being introduced after implementation.

Enterprise Legal Architecture exists to establish trust, accountability, ownership, transparency, and sustainability across the entire enterprise ecosystem.

Legal is therefore considered an enabling capability rather than a restrictive function.

---

# 5. Relationship to Enterprise Principles

Enterprise Legal Architecture adopts all Enterprise Principles defined in PRIN-000.

Applicable principles SHALL be interpreted within the legal domain while maintaining consistency with the Enterprise Foundation.

LEGAL-000 does not redefine Enterprise Principles.

# 7. Architecture Overview

Enterprise Legal Architecture establishes the legal foundation for the entire DevinaOS ecosystem.

Rather than functioning as an isolated legal department or a reactive compliance mechanism, Enterprise Legal Architecture is embedded across all enterprise domains, ensuring that legal considerations become an integral part of architecture, governance, decision-making, operations, and artificial intelligence.

The architecture governs the complete lifecycle of enterprise legal assets, rights, obligations, compliance requirements, ownership, digital assets, knowledge assets, and legal evidence.

Enterprise Legal Architecture applies to every enterprise capability, including:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Business Processes
- Enterprise Governance
- Policies and Standards
- Documentation
- Data and Information
- Software and Source Code
- Artificial Intelligence
- Digital Assets
- Contracts and Agreements
- Knowledge Management
- Enterprise Services

Legal Architecture therefore serves as the legal operating layer that enables trust, accountability, ownership, compliance, and sustainability throughout the enterprise lifecycle.

# 11. Enterprise Legal Governance

Enterprise Legal Architecture SHALL be governed through clearly defined roles, responsibilities, authorities, and governance processes.

Legal governance is established to ensure consistency, accountability, compliance, and sustainable evolution of the DevinaOS legal ecosystem.

## Governance Objectives

Enterprise Legal Governance aims to:

- Ensure legal consistency across the enterprise.
- Protect enterprise assets and intellectual property.
- Support enterprise decision-making.
- Enable trustworthy AI and automation.
- Reduce legal and compliance risks.
- Preserve enterprise legal knowledge.

---

## Governance Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise Architecture Board | Defines Enterprise Legal Architecture. |
| Legal Governance Owner | Owns the Legal Family documentation. |
| Domain Owner | Ensures legal compliance within each enterprise domain. |
| Document Owner | Maintains legal documentation. |
| AI Agent | Assists legal activities within approved governance boundaries. |
| Human Reviewer | Reviews and approves legal decisions and legal artifacts. |

---

## Governance Responsibilities

Enterprise Legal Governance is responsible for:

- Enterprise legal architecture.
- Legal documentation.
- Intellectual property.
- Licensing.
- Legal compliance.
- AI governance.
- Digital asset governance.
- Enterprise legal knowledge.
- Legal evidence.
- Legal risk oversight.

---

## Governance Authority

Only authorized governance bodies may:

- Approve Legal Architecture.
- Approve Enterprise Legal Policies.
- Approve Legal Standards.
- Approve Legal Frameworks.
- Approve Enterprise Legal Baselines.

Operational implementation MAY be delegated, but governance authority SHALL remain within Enterprise Governance.

# 12. Enterprise Legal Lifecycle

Every legal artifact SHALL follow a governed lifecycle.

```
Candidate
    ↓
Draft
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Approved
    ↓
Published
    ↓
Operational
    ↓
Revised
    ↓
Archived
    ↓
Retired
```

Unless otherwise specified, Enterprise Legal Artifacts SHALL follow the Enterprise Lifecycle Model.

## Lifecycle Activities

| Stage | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate | Initial proposal. |
| Draft | Under development. |
| Review | Subject matter review. |
| Approved | Governance approval completed. |
| Published | Official enterprise baseline. |
| Operational | Actively used. |
| Revised | Updated version under governance. |
| Archived | No longer active but retained. |
| Retired | Permanently withdrawn. |

Enterprise Legal Governance SHALL ensure complete traceability throughout the lifecycle.
