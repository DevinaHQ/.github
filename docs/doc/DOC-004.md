# DOC-004 — Document Relationship Standard

## Status

Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official relationship standard for all DevinaOS documentation.

It establishes the principles, relationship models, relationship categories, dependency rules, navigation standards, and traceability requirements that govern how official documents are connected throughout the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem.

This standard ensures that documentation forms an integrated knowledge network rather than isolated documents, enabling consistent governance, semantic navigation, AI-assisted interpretation, impact analysis, and long-term maintainability.

This standard complements DOC-001 — Documentation Metadata Standard, DOC-002 — Document Structure Standard, and DOC-003 — Document Writing Standard by defining how documents relate to one another.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to every official DevinaOS document, including but not limited to:

- Documentation (DOC)
- Philosophy (PHIL)
- Architecture (ARCH)
- Blueprint (BLUE)
- Information Map (MAP)
- Domain (DOMAIN)
- Capability (CAP)
- Module (MOD)
- Data (DATA)
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Standards
- Specifications
- Guidelines
- Policies
- Procedures
- References
- Catalogues
- Future official documentation

This standard governs logical relationships between documents. It does not define metadata, document structure, writing conventions, lifecycle, versioning, or naming standards, which are governed by their respective documents.

---

# Background

As the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem grows, isolated documentation becomes increasingly difficult to govern, maintain, search, and understand.

A standardized relationship framework enables structured navigation, dependency management, semantic traceability, architectural analysis, impact assessment, AI-assisted reasoning, and future automation.

Rather than existing as independent documents, every official DevinaOS document is considered part of an interconnected knowledge ecosystem.

---

# Relationship Principles

Every document relationship shall comply with the following principles.

- Single Source of Truth
- Explicit Relationships
- One Relationship, One Meaning
- Semantic Consistency
- Traceability
- Governance First
- Navigation by Design
- Platform Independence
- Scalability
- Long-Term Maintainability

---

# Relationship Philosophy

DevinaOS documentation is designed as an interconnected knowledge network.

Every official document shall participate in one or more meaningful relationships with other official documents.

Relationships shall express knowledge rather than merely provide hyperlinks.

Each relationship should communicate a clear semantic meaning that can be interpreted consistently by humans, AI assistants, automation systems, and future DevinaOS platforms.

Relationship definitions shall remain stable, predictable, and independent of any specific documentation platform.

---

# Relationship Model

Official document relationships are organized into the following conceptual models.

| Model | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Governance | Defines authority and governance relationships. |
| Structural | Defines hierarchical organization. |
| Dependency | Defines prerequisite or required relationships. |
| Reference | Connects supporting knowledge. |
| Complementary | Connects related concepts that enrich understanding. |
| Historical | Preserves document evolution and historical continuity. |
| Implementation | Connects conceptual documents to implementation artifacts. |

Each relationship shall belong to one primary relationship model.

---

# Relationship Categories

Relationships may be classified according to their functional purpose.

## Governance Relationships

Describe authority, ownership, policy, approval, or governance responsibilities.

Examples include governing documents, standards, policies, and architecture authorities.

---

## Structural Relationships

Describe parent-child organization within the documentation hierarchy.

These relationships define how documentation is organized.

---

## Dependency Relationships

Identify documents that must exist or be understood before another document can be interpreted or implemented.

Dependencies shall remain explicit.

---

## Reference Relationships

Connect documents that provide supporting, explanatory, or supplemental information.

Reference relationships do not establish authority.

---

## Complementary Relationships

Connect documents that expand understanding through related concepts without creating structural or dependency relationships.

---

## Historical Relationships

Preserve document succession, replacement, supersession, and long-term traceability.

Historical relationships shall never remove historical knowledge.

---

## Implementation Relationships

Connect conceptual documentation with implementation artifacts, operational procedures, software components, workflows, or future DevinaOS platform elements.

Implementation relationships support end-to-end traceability between governance and execution.

# Relationship Types

The following relationship types are officially recognized within the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem.

## Governs

Indicates that one document defines the rules, policies, or standards that another document shall follow.

Governance relationships establish authority.

Example:

```
DOC-000
   governs
DOC-006
```

---

## Parent Of

Identifies the immediate structural parent of another document.

Parent relationships define the official documentation hierarchy.

Each document should have only one structural parent unless explicitly approved through governance.

---

## Child Of

Represents the inverse relationship of **Parent Of**.

This relationship exists to improve navigation and traceability.

---

## Depends On

Indicates that one document requires another document as a prerequisite for interpretation, implementation, or governance.

Dependencies shall remain explicit.

Dependency does not imply ownership.

---

## References

Connects documents that provide supporting, explanatory, or contextual information.

Reference relationships are informative and do not establish governance authority.

---

## Complements

Connects documents that enhance understanding of the same subject from different perspectives.

Complementary relationships do not imply dependency.

---

## Implements

Connects conceptual documentation with implementation artifacts.

Typical implementation targets include:

- Procedures
- Work Instructions
- Software Modules
- Workflows
- Operational Processes
- Future DevinaOS Platform Components

---

## Supersedes

Indicates that a newer document officially replaces the authority of an older document.

The superseded document shall remain preserved for historical traceability.

---

## Replaces

Indicates that one document substitutes another operationally while historical governance remains unchanged.

Unlike **Supersedes**, replacement does not necessarily transfer governance authority.

---

## Related To

Represents a general semantic relationship where no more specific relationship type is appropriate.

This relationship should be used sparingly.

Whenever possible, a more explicit relationship type should be selected.

---

# Relationship Direction

Relationships shall be interpreted according to their defined direction.

Relationship direction shall remain semantically consistent throughout the documentation ecosystem.

Example:

```
ARCH-002
Depends On
ARCH-001
```

does not imply

```
ARCH-001
Depends On
ARCH-002
```

Relationship direction shall never be inferred solely from document position.

Explicit relationships always take precedence.

---

# Relationship Cardinality

Relationships may exist in one of the following cardinality models.

| Cardinality | Description |
| --- | --- |
| One-to-One | One document relates to exactly one other document. |
| One-to-Many | One document relates to multiple documents. |
| Many-to-One | Multiple documents relate to one governing or referenced document. |
| Many-to-Many | Multiple documents relate to multiple documents. |

Relationship cardinality shall accurately represent the semantic intent of the relationship.

---

# Relationship Rules

Relationships shall comply with the following rules.

- Every relationship shall have a clearly defined semantic meaning.
- Relationships shall be explicit.
- Duplicate relationships are prohibited.
- Circular governance relationships are prohibited.
- Circular structural hierarchies are prohibited.
- Dependency loops shall be avoided.
- Relationship direction shall remain consistent.
- Relationship types shall not be used interchangeably.
- Every relationship shall contribute to knowledge discovery or governance.

---

# Relationship Integrity

Relationship integrity shall be maintained throughout the entire document lifecycle.

The documentation ecosystem shall avoid:

- Orphan documents without meaningful relationships.
- Invalid parent-child structures.
- Circular governance hierarchies.
- Duplicate governing relationships.
- Broken references.
- Ambiguous semantic relationships.
- Conflicting dependency definitions.

Relationship integrity should be periodically reviewed as part of documentation governance to preserve consistency, traceability, and long-term maintainability.

# Traceability

Every official DevinaOS document shall be traceable through one or more meaningful relationships.

Traceability enables governance, architectural understanding, dependency analysis, impact assessment, AI-assisted reasoning, and future automation.

A typical conceptual traceability path within the DevinaOS ecosystem is illustrated below.

```
PHIL
   │
   ▼
ARCH
   │
   ▼
MAP
   │
   ▼
DOMAIN
   │
   ▼
CAP
   │
   ▼
MOD
   │
   ▼
DATA
```

This conceptual hierarchy represents the primary flow of architectural knowledge.

Additional semantic, governance, dependency, implementation, and reference relationships may exist across documents where appropriate.

Traceability shall support both top-down exploration and bottom-up impact analysis.

---

# Navigation Principles

Document relationships shall support efficient navigation throughout the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem.

Relationship design should enable:

- Governance navigation
- Architectural navigation
- Knowledge discovery
- Dependency analysis
- Impact assessment
- Cross-domain exploration
- AI-assisted navigation
- Future semantic search

Navigation should minimize isolated documents and encourage discoverability through meaningful relationships.

---

# Dependency Rules

Dependencies represent implementation, governance, architectural, or conceptual prerequisites.

Dependency relationships shall remain explicit and verifiable.

A dependency:

- Does not imply ownership.
- Does not imply governance authority.
- Does not automatically establish a structural relationship.

Dependency chains should remain as short and understandable as practical.

Circular dependencies shall be avoided.

---

# Relationship Governance

The creation, modification, and removal of document relationships shall follow the official DevinaOS governance process.

Relationship changes should preserve:

- Semantic consistency
- Architectural integrity
- Traceability
- Navigation quality
- Documentation maintainability

Significant changes to relationship definitions shall be reviewed before approval.

Relationship governance shall remain consistent with the principles defined by DOC-000 — Documentation Governance.

---

# Compliance

Every official DevinaOS document shall comply with this standard.

Documents that do not conform to the defined relationship model shall be updated before receiving **Approved** status.

Compliance with this standard strengthens governance, semantic consistency, traceability, AI interoperability, and long-term knowledge preservation throughout the DevinaOS ecosystem.

# Closing Statement

The DevinaOS Document Relationship Standard establishes the semantic foundation that connects every official document into a unified knowledge ecosystem.

By defining consistent relationship models, categories, rules, and governance, DevinaOS transforms documentation from isolated artifacts into an interconnected enterprise knowledge network that supports governance, navigation, traceability, AI reasoning, automation, and long-term maintainability.

The relationship model defined in this standard serves as a foundational capability for future DevinaOS platforms, enabling scalable knowledge management across documentation, architecture, implementation, and intelligent systems.
