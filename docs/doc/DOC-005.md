# DOC-005 — Document Lifecycle Standard

## Status

Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official lifecycle standard for all official DevinaOS documents.

It establishes the governance states, lifecycle stages, transition rules, review requirements, and historical preservation principles that govern every official document throughout its lifecycle.

This standard ensures that every document remains properly governed, accurate, traceable, maintainable, and authoritative from its initial creation through its eventual retirement.

This standard governs **document lifecycle states**.

Document version evolution is governed separately by **DOC-006 — Document Versioning Standard**.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to every official DevinaOS document, including but not limited to:

- Documentation (DOC)
- Philosophy (PHIL)
- Architecture (ARCH)
- Blueprint (BLUE)
- Information Map (MAP)
- Domain (DOMAIN)
- Capability (CAP)
- Module (MOD)
- Data (DATA)
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Standards
- Specifications
- Guidelines
- Policies
- Procedures
- References
- Catalogues
- Future official documentation

This standard governs document lifecycle states only.

Metadata, document structure, writing conventions, document relationships, versioning, and naming are governed by their respective standards.

---

# Background

Enterprise knowledge evolves continuously.

Without a standardized lifecycle, documents become inconsistent, outdated, duplicated, or disconnected from current governance and implementation.

A defined lifecycle enables controlled governance while preserving historical knowledge, maintaining trust in the documentation ecosystem, and ensuring that only authoritative documents guide future decisions.

---

# Lifecycle Principles

Every document lifecycle shall comply with the following principles.

- Governance First
- Controlled State Transitions
- One Document, One Lifecycle
- Traceability
- Historical Preservation
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Consistency
- Continuous Improvement
- Long-Term Maintainability

---

# Lifecycle Philosophy

Every official document progresses through a governed lifecycle while preserving its identity, authority, history, and traceability.

The lifecycle defines the **governance state** of a document.

Document evolution over time is governed independently through version management as defined by DOC-006 — Document Versioning Standard.

Lifecycle and versioning complement one another while serving different governance responsibilities.

---

# Lifecycle Stages

Every official document shall progress through the following governance lifecycle.

| Stage | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Initial document creation and authoring. |
| Review | Technical, architectural, editorial, and governance validation. |
| Approved | Formal governance approval has been granted. |
| Effective | Official active document governing current operations and decisions. |
| Superseded | Authority transferred to a newer approved document while preserving historical traceability. |
| Archived | Permanently retained for historical reference without active governance authority. |

Each lifecycle stage represents a governance state rather than a document version.

Only **Effective** documents shall be considered authoritative for operational, architectural, and governance purposes.

# Lifecycle Flow

The official lifecycle of every DevinaOS document shall follow the governance flow below.

```
Draft
   │
   ▼
Review
   │
   ▼
Approved
   │
   ▼
Effective
```

When changes are required, the **Effective** document remains the current authoritative document while a new revision is prepared as a separate working copy in accordance with **DOC-006 — Document Versioning Standard**.

The new working copy shall begin a new governance lifecycle.

```
Current Effective Document
            │
            ▼
     New Working Copy
            │
            ▼
         Draft
            │
            ▼
         Review
            │
            ▼
        Approved
            │
            ▼
        Effective
```

After the new version becomes **Effective**, the previous effective document transitions to **Superseded**.

```
Previous Effective
        │
        ▼
   Superseded
        │
        ▼
    Archived
```

This lifecycle preserves governance continuity while allowing controlled document evolution through independent version management.

# Stage Definitions

## Draft

A document undergoing initial creation or substantial redevelopment.

Draft documents are working documents and may change without formal governance approval.

Draft documents shall not be considered authoritative.

---

## Review

A document undergoing technical, architectural, editorial, and governance validation.

The Review stage verifies:

- Technical accuracy
- Architectural consistency
- Governance compliance
- Structural compliance
- Writing quality
- Metadata completeness
- Relationship integrity

Documents remain non-authoritative during Review.

---

## Approved

A document that has successfully completed the governance review process.

Approval confirms that the document satisfies all applicable standards and governance requirements.

Approved documents are eligible to become Effective.

---

## Effective

An officially active document that represents the current authoritative position of DevinaOS.

Only Effective documents shall govern:

- Architecture
- Documentation
- Standards
- Policies
- Procedures
- Operational guidance

An Effective document remains authoritative until formally superseded.

---

## Superseded

A document whose governance authority has been transferred to a newer Effective document.

Superseded documents remain available to preserve historical continuity, traceability, governance audits, and architectural evolution.

Superseded documents shall not be used as the primary operational reference.

---

## Archived

A document permanently retained for historical, legal, audit, or knowledge preservation purposes.

Archived documents no longer participate in active governance but remain part of the DevinaOS knowledge repository.

---

# Lifecycle Transition Rules

Lifecycle transitions shall follow the official governance sequence.

- Draft → Review
- Review → Approved
- Approved → Effective
- Effective → Superseded
- Superseded → Archived

Transitions outside this sequence require explicit governance approval.

The preparation of a new working copy is governed by **DOC-006 — Document Versioning Standard** and does not alter the lifecycle state of the current Effective document until the replacement has been approved and activated.

---

# Review Requirements

Before receiving **Approved** status, every document shall be reviewed for:

- Technical accuracy
- Architectural consistency
- Governance compliance
- Structural compliance
- Writing quality
- Metadata completeness
- Relationship integrity
- Cross-document consistency
- Reference validation

Review activities shall comply with the applicable governance standards defined within the DevinaOS documentation framework.

---

# Historical Preservation

Historical documents are considered permanent knowledge assets within the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem.

Superseded and Archived documents shall remain accessible for:

- Governance traceability
- Historical reference
- Architectural evolution
- Audit activities
- Knowledge preservation

Historical preservation supports long-term enterprise memory and ensures that significant governance decisions remain traceable throughout the evolution of DevinaOS.

# Compliance

Every official DevinaOS document shall comply with this lifecycle standard.

Documents that do not follow the defined lifecycle shall not receive **Approved** status.

Compliance with this standard ensures that every official document remains governed, traceable, maintainable, and authoritative throughout its lifecycle.

# Closing Statement

The DevinaOS Document Lifecycle Standard establishes the governance framework that defines how every official document progresses from creation to retirement.

By separating lifecycle governance from version evolution, DevinaOS ensures that document authority, traceability, historical preservation, and continuous improvement remain clear, consistent, and independently governed.

Together with the Document Versioning Standard, this lifecycle framework enables a controlled, scalable, and trustworthy documentation ecosystem capable of supporting enterprise governance, AI-assisted knowledge management, and the long-term evolution of DevinaOS.
