# Purpose

The Document Review Standard defines the official review process for all DevinaOS documentation.

Its purpose is to ensure consistency, completeness, quality, and architectural integrity before a document is formally approved.

---

# Objectives

The review process aims to:

- Ensure documentation quality.
- Verify compliance with documentation standards.
- Detect inconsistencies.
- Improve readability.
- Maintain architectural integrity.
- Prevent conflicting documentation.

---

# Review Principles

## Consistency

Every document shall comply with the official DevinaOS Documentation Standards.

## Completeness

All required sections shall be present and sufficiently documented.

## Accuracy

Documentation shall be technically, logically, and architecturally correct.

## Traceability

Review decisions shall be recorded and traceable.

## Constructive Feedback

Reviews should improve documentation quality through objective and actionable feedback.

---

# Review Types

## Editorial Review

Reviews grammar, spelling, formatting, and readability.

## Technical Review

Validates technical correctness and factual accuracy.

## Architecture Review

Ensures alignment with the DevinaOS Enterprise Architecture.

## Governance Review

Verifies compliance with DevinaOS governance principles, standards, and approved decisions.

---

# Review Checklist

Every review should verify at minimum:

- Metadata completeness
- Document ID correctness
- Naming compliance
- Structure compliance
- Writing quality
- Related Documents
- Architectural consistency
- Revision History
- Approval section
- Cross-reference validity

---

# Review Outcomes

A document review may result in one of the following outcomes:

## Approved for Approval

The document satisfies all review requirements and may proceed to approval.

## Minor Revision Required

Small corrections are required before approval.

## Major Revision Required

Significant revisions are required before another review.

## Rejected

The document does not satisfy the minimum documentation standards.

---

# Governance

Every official DevinaOS document shall complete the review process before entering the approval stage.

Review activities should be performed objectively and independently whenever practical.
