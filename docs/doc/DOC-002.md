# DOC-002 — Document Structure Standard

## Status

Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official structural standard for all Official Documents within the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem.

It establishes a consistent document organization that improves readability, governance, maintainability, interoperability, traceability, and long-term scalability across all official documentation.

This standard complements DOC-001 — Documentation Metadata Standard by defining the structure of document content rather than document metadata.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to every official DevinaOS document, including but not limited to:

- Documentation (DOC)
- Philosophy (PHIL)
- Architecture (ARCH)
- Blueprint (BLUE)
- Information Map (MAP)
- Domain (DOMAIN)
- Capability (CAP)
- Module (MOD)
- Data (DATA)
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Standards
- Specifications
- Catalogues
- References
- Future official documentation

This standard does not define metadata properties, writing style, document relationships, lifecycle, versioning, or naming conventions, which are governed by their respective standards.

---

# Background

As the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem continues to expand, maintaining a consistent document structure becomes essential for governance, navigation, knowledge preservation, AI interpretation, and long-term maintainability.

A standardized document structure enables contributors, reviewers, AI assistants, and future automation systems to understand, process, validate, and maintain documentation consistently.

This standard provides a common structural framework for every official DevinaOS document regardless of its subject or documentation platform.

---

Every official document shall comply with the following principles.

# Standard Document Structure

Every official document shall follow the official section order below.

| Order | Section | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Purpose | Yes |
| 2 | Scope | Yes |
| 3 | Background | Recommended |
| 4 | Core Sections | Yes |
| 5 | Governance | Yes |
| 6 | Related Documents | Yes |
| 7 | Version History | Optional |
| 8 | Change Log | Optional |
| 9 | Closing Statement | Yes |

The section order shall remain consistent unless explicitly approved through governance.

---

# Section Definitions

## Purpose

Defines why the document exists.

Every official document shall begin with a concise statement describing its objective.

---

## Scope

Defines what is included and excluded from the document.

The scope shall clearly establish document boundaries.

---

## Background

Provides the context explaining why the document is necessary.

Background shall describe the problem, governance need, or architectural context addressed by the document without repeating its purpose.

---

## Core Sections

Core Sections contain the primary knowledge defined by the document.

Their structure may vary depending on the document type while remaining consistent with the document purpose.

Core Sections should contain only information directly related to the document responsibility.

---

## Governance

Defines the authority, ownership, responsibilities, approval requirements, and governance applicable to the document.

Governance shall identify:

- Governing document.
- Change authority.
- Approval authority.
- Related governance standards.

---

## Related Documents

Identifies documents that govern, support, complement, or reference the current document.

Relationships should be identified whenever applicable.

Typical relationships include:

- Governing
- Depends On
- References
- Complements

Official document references shall be used whenever possible.

---

## Version History

Records official document versions and significant released revisions.

This section is optional and should be included only when version history is maintained within the document itself.

Document versioning shall comply with DOC-006 — Document Versioning Standard.

---

## Change Log

Provides a concise summary of significant changes introduced in each official document revision.

Typical entries may include:

- Version
- Date
- Summary of Changes
- Author
- Reviewer
- Approver

This section is optional.

Document revision management shall comply with DOC-006 — Document Versioning Standard.

---

## Closing Statement

Provides a concise statement summarizing the long-term intent of the document.

Every official DevinaOS document shall conclude with a Closing Statement that reinforces its purpose and long-term value within the DevinaOS documentation ecosystem.

---

# Structural Rules

Official documents shall comply with the following structural rules.

- Each section shall appear only once.
- Required sections shall not be omitted.
- Optional sections shall be included only when applicable.
- Section order shall remain consistent unless explicitly approved through governance.
- Headings shall follow the official Markdown heading hierarchy.
- Empty required sections are prohibited.
- Core Sections may vary according to the document purpose while preserving the overall document structure.
- Structural modifications require formal governance approval.
- Documents shall remain platform-independent and shall not rely on platform-specific formatting or features.

---

# Exceptions

Temporary working drafts, discussion documents, proposals, or collaborative working documents may adopt simplified structures during preparation.

Once an official document reaches **Approved** status, it shall fully comply with this standard before becoming part of the official DevinaOS documentation baseline.

Platform-specific presentation adjustments are permitted provided they do not alter the logical structure defined by this standard.

---

# Compliance

Every official DevinaOS document shall comply with this specification.

Documents that do not conform to the defined structure shall be updated before receiving **Approved** status.

Compliance with this standard contributes to consistent governance, improved readability, AI interoperability, maintainability, and long-term knowledge preservation across the DevinaOS ecosystem.
