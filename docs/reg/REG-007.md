# Document Authority

This document establishes the Enterprise Registry Relationship Standard of the Enterprise.

It defines the mandatory enterprise-wide standards governing relationships between Enterprise Registry Records across all Enterprise Registry Domains.

The Enterprise Registry Relationship Standard ensures that Enterprise relationships remain consistent, traceable, semantically meaningful, and governed throughout the Enterprise.

This standard inherits the Enterprise Registry Standard established by REG-001 and complements the Enterprise Registry Naming Standard established by REG-002, the Enterprise Registry Identifier Standard established by REG-003, the Enterprise Registry Lifecycle Standard established by REG-004, the Enterprise Registry Governance Standard established by REG-005, and the Enterprise Registry Reference Architecture established by REG-006.

This document does not define Registry Names, Registry Identifiers, Registry Lifecycle States, Registry Governance, Registry Classification, or implementation technologies.

Instead, it establishes the Enterprise Relationship Standards required to preserve Enterprise semantics, traceability, consistency, interoperability, and architectural integrity.

All Enterprise Registry Domains shall comply with this standard unless formally exempted through an approved Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

This document forms part of the Enterprise Standards Layer and serves as the authoritative standard governing Enterprise Registry Relationships.
