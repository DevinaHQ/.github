# Document Authority

This document establishes the Enterprise Registry Lifecycle Standard of the Enterprise.

It defines the mandatory enterprise-wide standards governing the lifecycle of every Enterprise Registry Record and Registry Domain.

The Enterprise Registry Lifecycle Standard ensures that every Registry Record progresses through a controlled, traceable, and governed lifecycle from creation through retirement.

This standard inherits the Enterprise Registry Standard established by REG-001 and complements the Enterprise Registry Naming Standard established by REG-002 and the Enterprise Registry Identifier Standard established by REG-003.

This document does not define Registry Names, Registry Identifiers, Registry Classification, Registry Relationships, Registry Governance, or implementation technologies.

Instead, it establishes the Enterprise Lifecycle Standards required to preserve Enterprise consistency, traceability, and governance throughout the lifecycle of every Registry Record.

All Enterprise Registry Domains shall comply with this standard unless formally exempted through an approved Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

This document forms part of the Enterprise Standards Layer and serves as the authoritative standard governing Enterprise Registry lifecycles.
