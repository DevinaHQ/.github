# Document Authority

This document establishes the Enterprise Registry Governance Standard of the Enterprise.

It defines the mandatory enterprise-wide governance standards governing every Enterprise Registry Record and Enterprise Registry Domain.

The Enterprise Registry Governance Standard ensures that Enterprise Registry decisions, authorities, responsibilities, approvals, compliance, and governance processes remain consistent, transparent, traceable, and accountable throughout the Enterprise.

This standard inherits the Enterprise Registry Standard established by REG-001 and complements the Enterprise Registry Naming Standard established by REG-002, the Enterprise Registry Identifier Standard established by REG-003, and the Enterprise Registry Lifecycle Standard established by REG-004.

This document does not define Registry Names, Registry Identifiers, Registry Lifecycle States, Registry Classification, Registry Relationships, or implementation technologies.

Instead, it establishes the Enterprise Governance Standards required to preserve Enterprise authority, accountability, consistency, compliance, and long-term architectural governance.

All Enterprise Registry Domains shall comply with this standard unless formally exempted through an approved Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

This document forms part of the Enterprise Standards Layer and serves as the authoritative standard governing Enterprise Registry Governance.
